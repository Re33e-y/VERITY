Division of labor

Each part does the one job it is good at

AI reasons
  • Reads the context and intent
  • Finds the approved evidence
  • Proposes minimal rewrites
  • Challenges its own findings
Code verifies
  • Exact numbers, dates and quantities
  • Explicit brand restrictions
  • Injection and restricted content
  • Re-checks every proposed patch
Humans govern
  • Approve, reject or revise
  • Decide every high-risk claim
  • Leave a reason for the record
  • Hold the final authority

Guardrails

When agents disagree, evidence wins

Verity never forces a consensus the evidence does not support. These rules are code, not prompts.

Agents disagree
The critic investigates. A vote does not decide.
A hard rule is violated
The violation is preserved. No model can clear it.
Evidence is missing
Marked unverifiable, never invented or assumed false.
A rewrite fails the re-check
The patch is rejected and never shown as a fix.
An agent cannot finish
A partial result returns with an explicit failure status.
A claim is high risk
Escalated to a qualified human reviewer.
